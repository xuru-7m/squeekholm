HOW IT WORKS

  • 200 floors, 8 biomes. Maps grow from 17×13 to 47×35. Every 10th floor is a boss floor whose stairs stay sealed until the boss falls; floor 200 hides the Cat Emperor and the golden wheel.
  • Deeper is deadlier and richer. Monsters get tougher and new kinds appear; chests roll better weapons, armor, slings and charms. Kill to gain XP and level up.
  • Fight two ways. Bump to melee, sling pebbles at range. Weapons can pierce, cleave, leech or crit. Linger too long on a floor and the burrow caves in.
  • The real hamster sets each run's mood from his certified work in SqueekCore's last 6 rounds: 💤 dark & drowsy, 🐾 normal, 🏃 richer, ⚡ deadlier but double seeds.
  • Ranked runs draw their burrow onchain (3 per Squeek per day). Save your floors any time with C: the contract replays every move of them and records your depth and score. Runs stay open for 2 days.
Sepolia testnet only. No tokens, no money: just seeds, glory and a real hamster.
